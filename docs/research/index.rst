Research
========

The research material records the technical evidence, assumptions, and open
validation work that guide Data Center Simulator. It evolves alongside the
methods and examples.

Current areas
-------------

* device power curves and equipment metadata;
* DC distribution, rack conversion, copper losses, and voltage limits;
* communication routing, offered traffic, and failures;
* air and liquid cooling, heat rejection, and thermal dynamics;
* generation, storage, grid limits, and supply shortfalls;
* model validation and future work on protection, redundancy, and telemetry.
