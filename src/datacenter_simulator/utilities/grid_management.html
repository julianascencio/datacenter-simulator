Wyoming: power, recovery and grid management

Evidence cutoff: 8 November 2026. Google acquired the site; operational success is not established.

Read these results correctly

Monthly planning and hourly operation are separate models. Planning uses MW/MWh, PUE 1.20 and a hypothetical 100 MW firm addition. The operational benchmark uses kW/kWh at one facility bus, synthetic 2030 demand and known future outages. Its 1 GWh / 200 MW battery is hypothetical. The utility path includes Black Hills generation and market import. All modeled gas generation shares one stress-test fuel dependency. No results are measured operator performance.

Economic dispatch includes generator ramps, capture auxiliaries, source-specific emissions, grid caps, finite storage, binary charge/discharge, terminal reserve and weighted critical/flexible shedding. It is not transmission power flow or transient islanding simulation. Flexible shedding is lost work.
