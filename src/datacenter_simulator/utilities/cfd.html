__MESH__

Model scope and reproducibility

    Supply enters a low slot on x=0. The whole x=L wall is an open pressure return. Other walls are adiabatic and no slip. Rack heat and resistance are distributed by geometric overlap. Constant mixing coefficients are configurable assumptions. This is an incompressible Boussinesq research solver; it does not resolve server fans, conjugate solid heat transfer or calibrated turbulence.

    python -m datacenter_simulator.analysis.cfd --inputs case-0-inputs.json --mesh-study --output outputs/my-cfd

    __LINKS__

    case-0 is normal flow; case-1, when present, is half flow. NPZ files contain all saved 3D frames. Open the VTK files in ParaView. Changing physical inputs requires rerunning Python; the report explores completed simulations.
