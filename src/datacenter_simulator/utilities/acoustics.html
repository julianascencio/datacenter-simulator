    Assumptions and reproducibility

    Unweighted sound-power spectra (dB re 1 pW) are propagated to sound-pressure levels (dB re 20 µPa). Sources add incoherently by energy. The indoor model combines direct spreading with a diffuse reverberant term. Source treatment reduces emitted power in every direction; it is not an obstacle diffraction model.

    Gaussian source offsets are independent between sources and correlated across bands. A common propagation offset applies to all sources and receivers. Background is fixed. Intervals use a reproducible Monte Carlo sample.

    This is a steady equivalent-level model. It does not calculate acoustic waveforms, tonal phase, transmission through walls, rack shielding, structureborne sound, ground interference or auralization. CFD pressure is not used as acoustic pressure.
